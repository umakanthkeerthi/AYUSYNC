# Nurse Agent - Deep Empowerment Plan

## Core Role
Orchestrates nursing workflows, home health logistics, and urgent triage queues by integrating directly with the hospital EHR.

## Advanced Capabilities & Deep AI Integration

### 1. Spatial & Route Optimization (Dynamic Triage)
For home-health nurses, the agent integrates with real-time traffic (Google Maps API) and weather data. If a High-Risk event triggers for a patient across town, the agent dynamically re-routes the entire fleet of nurses, calculating the optimal detour for the closest nurse with the right specialty, minimizing drive time and maximizing critical care time.

### 2. Ambient Clinical Documentation
When a nurse arrives at a patient's home, the agent transitions to an "Ambient Listener" on the nurse's mobile device. It listens to the nurse-patient interaction and autonomously generates structured FHIR clinical notes, extracts vitals mentioned, and pushes them to the EHR. This reduces the nurse's post-visit screen time to near zero.

### 3. Augmented Reality (AR) Wound Care Support
The agent integrates with the mobile camera or nurse smart-glasses. Using advanced Computer Vision, it analyzes a patient's wound in real-time, calculates surface area, detects necrotic tissue, and overlays AR guidelines for debridement or dressing, ensuring gold-standard care is delivered uniformly.

### 4. Predictive Shift-Staffing Augmentation
By listening to the Risk Prediction Agent's forecast of hospital-wide deteriorations, the Nurse Agent autonomously interfaces with the hospital's HR software to call in float-pool nurses hours *before* the actual wave of ICU admissions hits.
