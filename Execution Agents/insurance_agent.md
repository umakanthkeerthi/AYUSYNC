# Insurance Agent - Deep Empowerment Plan

## Core Role
Administrative support for billing, checking active policies, and pre-filling prior authorizations via Payer APIs.

## Advanced Capabilities & Deep AI Integration

### 1. LLM-Driven Denial Overrides (Automated Appeals)
Insurance companies frequently deny complex claims. If a denial is received, the agent instantly uses a specialized Legal/Medical LLM (trained on Medicare/Medicaid guidelines and payer-specific manuals) to draft a highly technical, citation-heavy appeal letter. It autonomously quotes the payer's own policy manual against them, attaching the Risk Predictor's severity scores to legally justify the medical necessity of the intervention.

### 2. Real-Time Benefit Optimization
Healthcare pricing is highly opaque. The agent continuously scans thousands of formulary tiers, discount cards (like GoodRx), and manufacturer coupons in milliseconds. It autonomously calculates the absolute cheapest combination of billing codes and pharmacy routing for the patient's specific socioeconomic profile, ensuring financial toxicity never prevents care.

### 3. Smart Contract Adjudication (Blockchain)
For progressive health networks and accountable care organizations, the agent utilizes blockchain smart contracts to instantly adjudicate and settle micro-claims between the hospital and the payer. When a Care Plan is executed and confirmed by the Event Bus, the smart contract automatically triggers payment without needing human medical coders or billing departments.

### 4. Predictive Risk Adjustment (HCC Coding)
As the Patient State Agent updates the clinical picture, the Insurance Agent constantly scans the unstructured data to autonomously assign Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) codes. It ensures the hospital is accurately compensated for the true complexity of the patient by capturing every chronic condition perfectly, maximizing legal revenue.
